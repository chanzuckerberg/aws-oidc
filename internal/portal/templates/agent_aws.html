Missing an account or role?
Access is granted through Okta Identity Governance (OIG). To request it:
  1. Log in to czi.okta.com.
  2. Click Request access to open the OIG flow.
  3. Search for AWS - CZI and select the account and role you need.
  4. Submit the request. Once approved, the account and role will appear in the list above.
